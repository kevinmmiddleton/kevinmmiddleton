## Hi, I'm Kevin.

I'm a Full Stack Product Manager who makes work easier for the people inside a company: integrations, internal platforms, and AI that fits into how teams already work. Engineer, then consultant, now 13 years in product.

I also build my own tools, which is how I know what AI can and can't do at work. Most of them I give away.

### Where I've done it

- **Lever:** built HRIS Sync, the enterprise integration that unblocked Lever's upmarket deals, with org-chart-aware approvals and automated user provisioning. 37% customer engagement in its first quarter.
- **Sendoso:** built the internal tools that took eGift refunds, swaps, and deactivations off engineering's plate, and doubled eGift coverage to 1,600 options in 47 countries.
- **CGI and CACI:** started as a C++ engineer on Momentum, the federal government's core financial system, then led its testing and go-live triage across six Department of Justice agencies, with budgets up to $200M.
- **Rocket Lawyer:** a co-branded partner platform reaching 11.5M users, with its own sign-in, pricing, and theming for each partner.
- **GridStrong:** an AI compliance copilot for power plant owners. My prototypes helped win a $1.4M DOE grant and fed a $10M seed round.
- **HVAC.com:** lifted conversion off a 0.1% baseline with high-intent tools. The calculators converted at 7.3%.
- **Oracle:** led a platform overhaul that shipped 36 features in 12 months and doubled the social networks it supported.
